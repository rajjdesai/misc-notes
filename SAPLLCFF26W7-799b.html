milboy (Milena) 104.22, RedditoLombardi (Parv) 95.48. 1.12 POINTS OF PURE ANXIETY

Rylia, someone on that co-managed team needs to check the lineup before the games start, and maybe it's the one who did all the heavy lifting at the draft. CeeDee Lamb's 21.9 was the only thing keeping this from being a war crime.
