🏈 WEEK 1 SEASON PREVIEW: RECEIPTS DUE, EXCUSES START MONDAY

Draft grades are in. Real games are not — yet. Scores below are all 0-0 because nobody's played a snap. That doesn't mean there's nothing to talk shit about.
THE MATCHUPS

Real Week 1 pairings, straight from Sleeper. Nobody's scored a point yet, so treat everything below as pre-game trash talk and things to watch, not predictions.

Katrina (B+) vs. Apurva (A-)

Katrina's "Minivan That Never Breaks Down" draft was boring and correct, and now she's rolling out Jalen Hurts as her floor play — except the floor just lost its engine. Hurts got dumped by A.J. Brown (yes, he's a Patriot now, more on that below) and is breaking in a brand new OC in Week 1. "Reestablish himself as an elite fantasy quarterback" is doing a lot of heavy lifting in that sentence, Katrina.

Across from her, Apurva — one of the two "Ice Queens" who drafted an A- — had the nerve to wait until Round 10 on quarterback and still landed a QB with zero live snaps in almost a month by kickoff: Jaxson Dart. He "feels in command," allegedly. Bold talk for someone who spent the entire draft refusing to blink.

Raj (B+) vs. Malvi (A-)

Raj graded his own draft a B+ — of course he did, he owns the curve — after reaching for Drake Maye a full round early in Round 4. Turns out the reach might actually cash: New England added both Romeo Doubs AND A.J. Brown this offseason to feed him. Funny thing is, Raj didn't draft Brown. Malvi did.

Malvi's A- "Ice Queen" build rostered three running backs behind Bijan Robinson and still somehow has Josh Allen, the consensus #1 fantasy quarterback, running the show. Only catch: Week 1 is the Texans, a top-6 pass defense that some analysts are flagging as Allen's best shot all year to actually get neutralized. Enjoy the receipts if it happens, Raj.

Parv (C-) vs. Cody (B-)

Parv's C- is the worst grade in the league for a reason — Ja'Marr Chase at 1.04 and Brian Thomas falling into her lap, then she torched it with a Kimani Vidal reach over 100 picks ahead of ADP and a Sam Darnold dart throw nobody asked for. At least Joe Burrow, fresh off turf-toe surgery and reportedly healthy for Week 1, is "a rock-solid QB1 with top-3 upside" throwing to Chase and Higgins. One good decision buried under two arson attempts.

Cody's B- came from genuine QB patience — waited to Round 12 — immediately undone by reaching on Travis Kelce three rounds early. His actual best starter is De'Von Achane, who Miami's own coach floated for "40+ touches a game" this year despite offseason shoulder surgery. Cody, for once, drafted the right guy on accident.

Lelia & Ryan (C) vs. Abhishek (C+)

Lelia & Ryan's C was CeeDee Lamb and Derrick Henry standing on business, undercut by a Jayden Reed reach 54 picks ahead of ADP and a tight end room that's one George Kittle hamstring away from total collapse. Now they're trotting out Lamar Jackson, who juggled hamstring, knee, ankle, and toe issues last year and got knocked down to 16.5 points per game for it. One analyst literally called him "a QB1 buy that needs an insurance plan." Checks out for this roster.

Abhishek's C+ "Allergic-to-Running-Backs Special" landed Trey McBride at pick 28 for nothing, then blew 50 picks of ADP on a rookie who hasn't played a single NFL snap. His saving grace is Justin Herbert, who draws the Cardinals' 24th-ranked pass defense in Week 1 with new OC Mike McDaniel calling plays. Somebody in this matchup is getting exposed early — smart money says it isn't the Chargers QB.

Milena (B) vs. CJ (A)

Milena's B was "Perfectly Mid" by design, and the whole grade hinges on whether reaching for Caleb Williams 17 picks early in Round 6 was vision or vibes. Week 1 leans vision, maybe — the Panthers had the second-worst third-down defense in football last year, and Williams is being called live to lead all players in Week 1 scoring if Ben Johnson's offense hits like advertised.

And then there's CJ. The A grade. The Heist of the Century. Best value per pick in the entire league, and he STILL wasn't done — Jayden Daniels was basically free in Round 7 and is reportedly having his strongest training camp yet, even after last year's knee/hamstring/elbow tour and an offensive line that just lost Laremy Tunsil to a torn triceps. If that line holds up even a little, Milena's about to learn what an actual heist looks like from the other sideline.

  • Parv's Kimani Vidal reach becomes this league's cautionary campfire story by Thanksgiving.

  • One of the Round-10-or-later quarterbacks (looking at you, Apurva and Cody) finishes as a top-5 fantasy QB and ruins everyone's faith in ADP forever.
  • The Toilet Bowl gets decided by a Week 15 tiebreaker, not talent.
