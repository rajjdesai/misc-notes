FIRST TIME PLAYING? READ THIS

Set your lineup before kickoff. Each player locks individually at THEIR OWN game's start time — not a single league-wide deadline. Week 1's first three kickoffs:

Wed 9/98:20 PM ETPatriots @ Seahawks
Thu 9/108:35 PM ET49ers vs Rams (Melbourne)
Sun 9/131:00 PM ETearly Sunday slate begins

A few of you have never done this before. Here's what else will actually bite you if you don't know it going in:

  • Lineups lock per player, not per team. Only players in games that have started lock — you can still swap guys playing later in the day right up until their own kickoff.
  • Waivers are rolling, no FAAB. Claims clear Wednesday 3AM ET on a 2-day window. Anyone truly unowned can be added instantly, first-come-first-served, any day.
  • This is a half-PPR league — 0.5 points per reception, not the full 1.0 you may be used to from other leagues.
  • Defenses score a flat ZERO if they allow 21–27 points. Don't autopilot a defense start — check the matchup.
  • Trade deadline is Week 11. No need to panic-trade in September; you've got time.
  • Top 6 make the playoffs, starting Week 15. Miss it, and you're playing for pride — or the Poop Bowl.
  • Last place becomes Poop King/Queen and owes a combined 20 (guys) / 15 (girls) across beers, miles run, and pizza slices at the group event. Don't finish last.
THE MATCHUPS

Five real Week 1 pairings. Scores are all zero right now — that's not permission to be nice about it.

Joe Burrow
VS

Parv's draft was an arson investigation and Joe Burrow — fresh off turf-toe surgery — is the one firefighter left standing. Cody waited on QB like a disciplined adult and then blew the savings on a washed Travis Kelce three rounds early anyway. At least Achane's own coach is promising 40 touches a game, so somebody on this roster still has a pulse.

Abhishek's running back room is four deep and one of them is a literal rookie who's never played a down, so pray for Justin Herbert, who at least draws a bad Cardinals pass D in Week 1.

Some people don't deserve nice things.

THE LONG CON

Forget Week 1 for a second. This is what's actually going to decide your season — the shit you built (or didn't) back in August.

Raj

Six running backs deep means this roster is basically bye-week-proof and injury-proof all season — annoyingly correct sequencing from a guy who graded his own draft a B+ like he was being humble about it.

Malvi

Three running backs behind Bijan is either genius depth or a full-blown panic attack in roster form. Ask again the week Bijan actually misses a game.

Milena

No real holes, no real ceiling — "Perfectly Mid" was the nickname and it's staying accurate all 17 weeks unless Caleb Williams pulls off a miracle nobody's actually banking on.

Parv

Elite receivers, catastrophic everything else — she built a Ferrari and then set the engine on fire before the season even started.

Katrina

JSN and Jeanty give her a real floor no matter what Hurts does this year, which is the only reason this roster isn't a full-blown disaster.

Cody

An elite Achane/Barkley combo, torched by three rounds wasted on a corpse named Travis Kelce — some mistakes just cost you all season long.

Abhishek

Trey McBride at pick 28 was a genuine robbery, but a four-man running back room with a rookie who's never taken an NFL snap is going to be a war of attrition by November.

Lelia & Ryan

One (1) tight end, made entirely of glass. If George Kittle so much as looks at his hamstring wrong, this whole operation collapses.

Apurva

Puka Nacua's a legit WR1, but the running back room behind Kenneth Walker is a committee nobody's excited about — that's the actual problem all season, not the QB.

CJ

Best value draft in the league, full stop. The only real risk is Christian McCaffrey's medical chart, and even then the bench (Swift, a nearly-free Jayden Daniels) covers for it.
