Kickoff is Wednesday. Here's what's actually worth knowing before then.
THE MATCHUPS

Five real Week 1 pairings — and what actually matters about each roster past this week.

Katrina vs. Apurva

Katrina's leaning on Jalen Hurts, who just lost A.J. Brown to the Patriots and is breaking in a brand-new offensive coordinator in Week 1 — but her Jaxon Smith-Njigba/Ashton Jeanty combo gives her a real floor even if the QB spot underwhelms. Apurva's actual season-long risk isn't Jaxson Dart (who reportedly "feels in command" already) — it's the running back room behind Kenneth Walker, a committee nobody's excited to start come Week 12.

Raj vs. Malvi

Raj's early reach on Drake Maye might actually cash — New England added both Romeo Doubs and A.J. Brown to feed him — and six total running backs make his roster close to bye/injury-proof all season. Malvi's stacked WR room (a Parker Washington heist included) is the envy of the league, but she's leaning on THREE running backs behind Bijan Robinson; if he's ever out for real, that depth becomes the whole story — starting with a tough Texans defense in Week 1.

Parv vs. Cody

Parv's Chase/Thomas WR duo is legitimately elite, but reaches on Kimani Vidal and Sam Darnold left the rest of her roster thin — Joe Burrow, fresh off turf-toe surgery and reportedly a "rock-solid QB1," is the one thing keeping this team's season afloat. Cody's Achane/Barkley combo is an elite floor and real QB patience freed up draft capital — but a Travis Kelce reach three rounds early is dead weight he'll be carrying all year. Achane's own coach floated 40+ touches a game, shoulder surgery notwithstanding.

Lelia & Ryan vs. Abhishek

Lelia & Ryan have real bones — CeeDee Lamb and Derrick Henry — but their tight end room is one George Kittle hamstring away from collapse, and now they're leaning on Lamar Jackson, who battled a four-body-part injury tour last year and still dropped to 16.5 points a game for it. Abhishek's Trey McBride heist at pick 28 was real theft, but his running back corps — four total, one a rookie who's never played an NFL snap — is paper-thin for a 17-week season. His best hope short-term is Justin Herbert, who draws Arizona's 24th-ranked pass defense in Week 1 under new OC Mike McDaniel.

Milena vs. CJ

Milena's build has no glaring holes and a genuinely stable floor — the whole season hinges on whether reaching for Caleb Williams was vision or vibes, and a shaky Panthers pass defense in Week 1 is the first real test. CJ is still riding the best-value draft in the league by a mile — his only real crack is Christian McCaffrey's medical file, but even in a down year there, a bench stocked with D'Andre Swift and a nearly-free Jayden Daniels covers for it.

FIRST TIME PLAYING? READ THIS

A few of you have never done this before. Here's what'll actually bite you if you don't know it going in:

  • Lineups lock per player, not per team. When the early games kick off Sunday, only THOSE players lock — you can still swap guys playing later in the day right up until their own kickoff.
  • Waivers are rolling, no FAAB. Claims clear Wednesday 3AM ET on a 2-day window. Anyone truly unowned can be added instantly, first-come-first-served, any day.
  • This is a half-PPR league — 0.5 points per reception, not the full 1.0 you may be used to from other leagues.
  • Defenses score a flat ZERO if they allow 21–27 points. Don't autopilot a defense start — check the matchup.
  • Trade deadline is Week 11. No need to panic-trade in September; you've got time.
  • Top 6 make the playoffs, starting Week 15. Miss it, and you're playing for pride — or the Poop Bowl.
  • Last place becomes Poop King/Queen and owes a combined 20 (guys) / 15 (girls) across beers, miles run, and pizza slices at the group event. Don't finish last.
BOLD PREDICTIONS

Purely speculative — ask again in Week 12.

  • CJ's historically efficient draft doesn't just make the playoffs — it wins the whole thing, wire to wire.
  • One of this year's "panic" late-round quarterbacks (Dart or Herbert) finishes as a top-3 fantasy QB, not just top-5 — and rewrites how this league drafts the position next year.
  • A team with a real bench crisis right now (Lelia & Ryan's tight end room, Abhishek's running back corps) misses the playoffs entirely because of it.
  • The Toilet Bowl comes down to the final possession of Week 17, not a tiebreaker.
