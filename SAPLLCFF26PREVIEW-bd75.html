🏈 WEEK 1 SEASON PREVIEW: RECEIPTS DUE, EXCUSES START MONDAY

Kickoff is Wednesday. Here's what's actually worth knowing before then.
THE MATCHUPS

Five real Week 1 pairings, five real storylines worth watching — not just a rerun of who got an A on draft day.

Katrina vs. Apurva

Katrina's leaning on Jalen Hurts, who just lost A.J. Brown to the Patriots and is breaking in a brand-new offensive coordinator in Week 1 — rough spot for a "safe" pick. Apurva counters with Jaxson Dart, who "feels in command" according to the hype despite barely taking a live snap all preseason. Confidence is easy to talk; Week 1 is where it gets tested.

Raj vs. Malvi

Raj's early reach on Drake Maye might actually cash — New England added both Romeo Doubs and A.J. Brown to feed him this offseason, and funny enough, Malvi's the one who drafted Brown, not Raj. Her counter is Josh Allen, the consensus #1 fantasy QB, but he opens against a Texans defense some analysts are calling his toughest matchup of the entire year.

Parv vs. Cody

Parv's roster is mostly wreckage, but Joe Burrow — fresh off turf-toe surgery and reportedly a "rock-solid QB1" throwing to Chase and Higgins — is the one thing holding it together. Cody's best asset is De'Von Achane, whose own coach floated 40+ touches a game despite offseason shoulder surgery. One good pick apiece, buried under everything else both of them did.

Lelia & Ryan vs. Abhishek

Lelia & Ryan are rolling out Lamar Jackson, who battled a four-body-part injury tour last year and still dropped to 16.5 points a game for it — one analyst called him "a QB1 buy that needs an insurance plan." Abhishek counters with Justin Herbert, who draws Arizona's 24th-ranked pass defense under new OC Mike McDaniel. Somebody's getting exposed early, and it might not be the Chargers guy.

Milena vs. CJ

Milena's reach on Caleb Williams could pay off fast — some are calling him to lead all players in Week 1 scoring if Ben Johnson's offense clicks the way it's supposed to. CJ, still riding the best draft in the league, somehow also landed Jayden Daniels basically for free — reportedly having his best camp yet, even after his own offensive line just lost a starting tackle to a torn triceps.

FIRST TIME PLAYING? READ THIS

A few of you have never done this before. Here's what'll actually bite you if you don't know it going in:

  • Lineups lock per player, not per team. When the early games kick off Sunday, only THOSE players lock — you can still swap guys playing later in the day right up until their own kickoff.
  • Waivers are rolling, no FAAB. Claims clear Wednesday 3AM ET on a 2-day window. Anyone truly unowned can be added instantly, first-come-first-served, any day.
  • Passing TDs are worth 6, not the usual 4. That's a real scoring quirk of this league — QB matchups swing points here more than you're used to.
  • Defenses score a flat ZERO if they allow 21–27 points. Don't autopilot a defense start — check the matchup.
  • Trade deadline is Week 11. No need to panic-trade in September; you've got time.
  • Top 6 make the playoffs, starting Week 15. Miss it, and you're playing for pride — or the Poop Bowl.
  • Last place becomes Poop King/Queen and owes a combined 20 (guys) / 15 (girls) across beers, miles run, and pizza slices at the group event. Don't finish last.
