POLAND FALLS... TO ITS OWN BENCH
Katrina 🇯🇵 (Japan) 102.56, Raj 🇵🇱 (Poland) 95.62

Your commissioner, Raj 🇵🇱 (Poland), was first to get rolled - historically accurate. Katrina 🇯🇵 (Japan) beat him 102.56 to 95.62 on the back of Ashton Jeanty (25.4) and C.J. Stroud (21.46). But Katrina didn't really beat Raj. Raj's bench beat Raj. Saquon Barkley (13.5) sat while Ollie Gordon II put up 7.7 at FLEX; Deebo Samuel (6.2) sat while Luther Burden III put up 2.3. Make those two moves and Poland wins 105.32 to 102.56. The man literally asked in the group chat whether there would ever be a week he doesn't leave a pile of points on his bench. Not this week, Commissioner. Not this week.

Katrina 🇯🇵 wasn't exactly running a flawless war room herself: Tyler Bass dropped 17 on her bench while Ka'imi Fairbairn kicked 6, and she started a Bucs defense that scored a perfect 0.0. Raj called her Axis from day one, and she's right there at 6-5. Possum behavior.
