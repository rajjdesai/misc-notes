The Breakdown
Jaylen Warren

sportsmaxxing treen (Katrina) 140.92, Cehehe (CJ) 93.66. Jaylen Warren's 33.8 led the charge in a 47.26-point beatdown that snaps CJ's run at 4-0. This one lived a whole life in the group chat before it ended: CJ watched his win probability swing from 61% to 2% ("Fuck sleeper"), claw back to 98% on a "BELIEVE" Ted Lasso meme, then collapse to 3% after Jayden Daniels and the Commanders marched into chip-shot field goal range in overtime against the Giants and just... let the clock run out, untouched, no kick attempted.
